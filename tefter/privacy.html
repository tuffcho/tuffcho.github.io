Privacy policy — Tefter

Last changed: 12 November 2026 · Dimitar Ganchev

In short

Tefter is an app for keeping track of your own income and expenses. It does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What data the app keeps

Your records (amounts, dates, categories, notes), categories, subscriptions, loans, budgets, savings and settings, in all accounts (the separate sets of books in the app). They are stored only in the app’s private folder on your phone, encrypted with a key that Android keeps on the same phone. The access code is stored only as a protected fingerprint (hash) from which the code itself cannot be recovered, plus its first digit (encrypted) as a hint if the code is forgotten. The backup password is not stored: only an encrypted key made from it is kept (and one made from the code, for copies while there is no password).

Internet, ads and tracking

The app does not use the internet. There are no ads, no behaviour analytics, no tracking, no profiles and no sign-up. The accounts in the app exist only on the phone; they are not online profiles. No personal data such as name, email, phone number or location is collected.

Third parties

We do not pass any data to third parties, because we do not receive any data ourselves. The developer has no access to your records.

Buying the full version

The full version is bought once through Google Play. The payment is handled entirely by Google under its terms and privacy policy. The developer receives no data about your payment: the app only asks Google Play on the phone whether the full version has been bought.

Backups and export

Backups are saved in the Documents/Tefter folder on the phone and are encrypted with your backup password (AES-256). Without the password they cannot be opened. A copy in the cloud (for example Google Drive) exists only if you save it there yourself. Exported tables (Excel, CSV, PDF) are not encrypted, so keep them like any personal document. The app does not use Android backup to your Google account.

Permissions

The app does not ask for special permissions. The only one is for unlocking with biometrics (fingerprint or face): the check is done by Android, and the app never sees or stores the fingerprint or face; it only gets a “yes” or “no”. The nightly copy to Documents/Tefter uses Android’s standard mechanism for background work.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your control and deleting your data

The data is yours. You can change or delete it at any time: single records, everything at once with “Settings → Reset”, or by uninstalling the app. Copies in Documents/Tefter, Google Drive or elsewhere are deleted by you.

Security

The data is in the app’s protected private folder and is encrypted. If you like, you can turn on a lock with biometrics (fingerprint or face) and a 6-digit backup code. The quick-expense widget shows no records or totals.

Disclaimer

The app is a tool for personal record keeping. It does not handle real money and makes no transfers, orders or payments. It is not financial, tax or accounting advice. Check important amounts yourself. The author is not responsible for decisions made on the basis of the data in the app, nor for improper use of the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and at tuffcho.github.io/tefter/privacy.html, with the date of the last change.
