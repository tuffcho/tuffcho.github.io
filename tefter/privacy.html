Privacy Policy — Tefter

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Dimitar Ganchev

Summary

Tefter does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

The financial entries you create are stored only on your device, inside the app. The developer cannot see them.

Backups and deletion

Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. If the system backup of your device is enabled, Android may include app data in your own Google backup; that is controlled by you and Google, not by the developer.

Third parties

The app is distributed through Google Play. Downloads and purchases (including the paid version) are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children and knowingly collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email tuffcho@gmail.com.
