What it does

🧮

Records in seconds

Amount, category – done. Built-in calculator (+ − × ÷), a cursor between the digits, records for past days too.

💶

Your available money at a glance

How much you have today and how much is left after subscriptions until the end of the month.

📊

Reports

Income, expenses and balance by month and year, expenses by category with percentages, average spending per day.

🎯

Budgets

An overall budget and budgets per category. A warning at 80 % and over the limit; leftovers can carry over.

🔁

Subscriptions and loans

Payments are recorded by themselves on their date. See upcoming payments and what is left on your loans.

💰

Savings

Pots with a goal and a deadline. Money in them is not an expense and stays apart from your available money.

👥

Several accounts

E.g. personal and family money kept apart, with transfers between them.

📤

Export

Tables in Excel, CSV and PDF – for a month, a year or all time.

🔐

Security

Lock with fingerprint, face or a 6-digit code. The data on the phone is encrypted.

🗂️

Backups

A copy every night in Documents/Tefter, protected by a password. Save it to Google Drive if you like.

⚡

Widget

A quick expense from the home screen without opening the app.

🌓

Light and dark theme

The app takes the colours of your phone (Android 12+).

Videos

Short demos of the full-version features (in Bulgarian, no sound).
