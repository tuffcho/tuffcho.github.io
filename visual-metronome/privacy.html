Privacy Policy — Visual metronome

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Dimitar Ganchev

Summary

Visual metronome does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

The songs and playlists you create (name, time signature, beat grouping, strong beats, tempo) and your settings are stored only on your device. The app has no internet access, so nothing can be sent anywhere. The developer cannot see your data.

Backups and deletion

Backups and exports (JSON backup, Excel, PDF) are created only when you ask for them, and only in the place you choose – a file or, for the automatic backup, a folder you pick. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device; your own backup and export files stay where you saved them. If the system backup of your device is enabled, Android may include app data in your own Google backup; that is controlled by you and Google, not by the developer.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps – to show the floating metronome bar on top of other apps. It is only shown when you turn it on.
  • Notifications and foreground service – while the floating bar is open, Android requires a notification; it lets you close the bar.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.

Third parties

The app is distributed through Google Play. Downloads are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and knowingly collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
