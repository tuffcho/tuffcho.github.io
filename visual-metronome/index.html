Visual metronome

A metronome you can see. A small see-through bar sits on top of sheet music, lyrics or any app and flashes every beat – red for strong beats, green for the rest.

What it does

👀

A beat you can see

One light per beat. Strong beats flash red, the others green – no need to hear a click on stage.

🪟

On top of any app

A small see-through bar floats over your sheet music, lyrics or PDF. Drag it anywhere, make it bigger or smaller, turn it 90°.

👆

Simple gestures

Tap the dots to stop / start, tap 3 times to close, hold 2 seconds to be asked. Tap the tempo to jump back to the app.

🥁

Asymmetric meters

5/8, 7/8, 9/8, 11/8, 13/8, 15/8… Ready-made groupings like 2+2+3 or 3+2+2, or type your own.

🔴

Strong beats your way

Tap any beat to make it strong. Several strong beats, a strong beat that is not the first, or none at all.

🎵

Saved songs

Each song keeps its name, time signature, grouping, strong beats and tempo. Edit it once – it changes everywhere.

📋

Playlists

Build a set list from your songs. Start it and the bar appears; the arrows switch to the next song, its name is shown below.

🎚️

Tempo 20–300 BPM

Arrow buttons (hold to go faster), a slider and tap tempo.

🔈

Soft sounds, if you want

Off by default. Soft click, wood or bell; strong beats sound different.

🗂️

Backups

A backup file, or an automatic backup to a folder after every change – each one checked after saving. Restore in one tap.

📤

Export

Your songs and playlists as an Excel table or a PDF.

🌓

Light and dark theme

And the screen stays on while the metronome plays.

🔒 Your songs stay with you

Visual metronome does not collect, send or sell any data. It does not even use the internet. There is no account and no ads – your songs and playlists are kept on your phone, and backups only where you put them.
